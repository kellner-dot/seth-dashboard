KaviBOT — Privacy Policy

KaviBOT is a personal, self-hosted AI assistant. It runs on the owner's own computer and connects to the owner's own Google Calendar using the read-only calendar scope.

What data is accessed

Calendar event titles, times, and descriptions, read-only, solely to answer the owner's questions about their schedule (e.g. "what's on my calendar tomorrow").

What is stored

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in the operating system's credential vault. No calendar data is uploaded, shared, sold, or sent to any third party.

Contact

This is a personal project with a single user. There is no data collection beyond what is described above.
