Passman

Sign-in launcher — pick an account to open its login page.
🛡️ Zero secrets here. Passman never stores, sees, or displays passwords.
To edit this list, update passman/accounts.json in the seth-dashboard repo. Names and login-page URLs only — never usernames or passwords.

How to sign in on the TV

  1. Pick an account below — its login page opens here.
  2. On your iPhone, open the Passwords app and copy the password.
  3. Open the phone remote page and use clipboard share to paste it onto the TV.
  4. Your passwords live on your phone only — this TV never keeps them.
